Utilities in Dallas

Who actually serves a Dallas address.

Dallas is a deregulated electricity market, which means you choose your provider. That choice is where most people overpay. We walk you through it.

  • ElectricityDeregulated. Oncor delivers, you pick the plan
  • Internet and TVAT&T Fiber, Spectrum, Frontier by address
  • Water and sewerCity of Dallas Water Utilities
  • Natural gasAtmos Energy, where applicable
  • TrashSet up with your city service
